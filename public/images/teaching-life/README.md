# Teaching Life photographs

One photograph per story in `src/data/teachingStories.ts`:

```ts
{ id: 'singapore-field-trip', …,
  photo: '/images/teaching-life/singapore.jpg',
  photoAlt: 'MBA students at the port of Singapore' }
```

Landscape, at least 1600px wide, originals rather than social-media copies.

Note that a story is a photograph **and** a piece of writing — a photo on
its own will render, but the section is meant to carry the account with it.
